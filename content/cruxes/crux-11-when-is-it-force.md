title: "When is it force?"
type: value

**What you're really disagreeing about:** whether pressure short of an actual mandate counts as coercion.

**How each side would put it:**

> "Nobody was forced. There was no law, no penalty, no one was arrested. If you could say no and accept the consequences, that's a choice, even if it was a hard one."

> "A choice between compliance and losing your job isn't a choice in any way that matters. Calling it voluntary because there's no statute is a technicality that lets people apply enormous pressure and deny doing it."

**Where it shows up:** employment conditions, vaccine and testing requirements, social pressure, contracts, plea bargains, parenting — "he chose to, I just explained what would happen otherwise."

**The test:**

1. **What was the cost of saying no, concretely?** Not "there were consequences" — name them. A raised eyebrow and losing your income are both consequences and nobody thinks they're the same.
2. **Where's your line?** Everyone has one. A fine is force to almost nobody; losing your home is force to almost everyone. Say roughly where yours sits, then compare.
3. **Who applied the pressure — the state, an employer, or other people?** Many disagreements are only about this. Two people can fully agree that a pressure was severe and disagree entirely about whether the source has the standing to apply it.
4. **Would you call it force if the pressure ran the other way?** The most reliable check in this whole crux. Try it before you answer the others.

**A worked example.** An argument about a workplace requirement. Both answer question 2 with a rough threshold. One says force begins at any material financial penalty, the other at physical compulsion. Every downstream disagreement follows from that gap, and neither position is absurd.

**Where it ends:** if your thresholds differ and hold up under question 4, you disagree about how much pressure a free choice can absorb before it stops being free. That's a genuine philosophical divide with a long, unresolved literature behind it.
